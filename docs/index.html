Parterre

A calm place to work with AI. Put AI helpers, terminals and little gardens side by side on your Mac.

Download for Mac

Free · macOS on Apple silicon (M1 or newer) · updates itself

A garden of tiles

Each tile is a bed: an AI working in a folder while you watch, your own terminal, or just a garden that grows while you work.

AI, side by side

Run Claude Code, Codex, Gemini or any AI tool in a folder. A few at once, each in its own bed, each labelled.

Arrange it your way

Columns or a grid, or drag a tile onto another's edge to split it. Drag the path between them to resize.

Gardens that grow

Plant flowers, trees and lawns. Water them, add compost, dig a pond. Squirrels, frogs and robins come to visit.

Weather and seasons

Morning light, rain, fog and fireflies at night. Blossom in spring, gold leaves in autumn, snow in winter.

Nothing is lost

Set a tile aside and it keeps running. Put it in the Shed and it waits, conversation and all, until you bring it back.

On all your Macs

Keep your gardens in iCloud Drive if you like. No account, no server, no tracking.

About the AI, plainly

  • Parterre has no AI of its own and sends nothing anywhere.
  • An AI session runs a tool already on your Mac, on your own account: Claude Code to start, or another you prefer.
  • What you ask it, and the files it reads in that folder, go to that company so it can answer. It works only in the folder you pick, and asks before changing things.
  • Gardens and terminals need no AI at all.
